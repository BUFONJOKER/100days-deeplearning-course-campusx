
# Artificial Neural Networks (ANN) – Practice & Concepts

This folder contains **step-by-step Jupyter notebooks** covering **Artificial Neural Networks (ANN)** concepts, starting from the basic perceptron to advanced optimization and regularization techniques.  
The notebooks focus on **theory + intuition + practical implementation using Python and Keras**.


---

## 📘 Notebook Descriptions

### 🔹 Fundamentals
- **01_perceptron_trick.ipynb**  
  Understanding the perceptron trick and decision boundaries.

- **02_loss_function_perceptron.ipynb**  
  Role of loss functions in perceptron learning.

- **03_problem_with_perceptron.ipynb**  
  Limitations of single-layer perceptrons (e.g., XOR problem).

---

### 🔹 Real-World ANN Applications
- **04_customer_churn_prediction.ipynb**  
  Binary classification using ANN for customer churn prediction.

- **05_handwrittendigits_classification.ipynb**  
  Multiclass classification of handwritten digits.

- **06_graduate_admission_regression.ipynb**  
  Regression problem using ANN to predict admission chances.

---

### 🔹 Optimization Techniques
- **07_batch_vs_stochastic_gradient_descent.ipynb**  
  Comparison of Batch, Stochastic, and Mini-batch Gradient Descent.

- **08_vanishing_gradient_problem.ipynb**  
  Explanation and impact of vanishing gradients in deep networks.

- **09_early_stopping.ipynb**  
  Preventing overfitting using early stopping.

- **10_feature_scaling.ipynb**  
  Importance of normalization and standardization in ANN training.

---

### 🔹 Regularization & Generalization
- **11_dropout_layer_classification.ipynb**  
  Dropout technique applied to classification problems.

- **12_dropout_layer_regression.ipynb**  
  Dropout usage in regression-based neural networks.

- **13_regularization.ipynb**  
  L1 and L2 regularization to reduce overfitting.

---

### 🔹 Weight Initialization Techniques
- **14_zero_weight_initialization.ipynb**  
  Why zero initialization fails in neural networks.

- **15_zero_point5__weight_initialization.ipynb**  
  Effects of constant weight initialization.

- **16_xavier_normal_weight_initialization.ipynb**  
  Xavier (Glorot) normal initialization.

- **17_he_normal_he_uniform_glorot_uniform_normal_weight_initialization.ipynb**  
  Comparison of He Normal, He Uniform, and Glorot initializations.

---

### 🔹 Advanced ANN Techniques
- **18_batch_normaliztion.ipynb**  
  Batch Normalization for faster and stable training.

- **19_exponentially_weighted_moving_average.ipynb**  
  EWMA for smoothing loss curves and optimization stability.
